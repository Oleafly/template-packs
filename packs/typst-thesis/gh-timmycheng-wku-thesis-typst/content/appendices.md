## Appendix B: Interview Protocols

### B.1 Semi-Structured Interview Guide

**Introduction (5 minutes)**
- Thank participant for their time
- Explain the purpose of the interview
- Obtain consent for recording
- Assure confidentiality

**Background and Context (10 minutes)**
1. Can you tell me about your general experience with online learning platforms?
2. What are your typical study habits and preferences?
3. How do you usually find and select learning materials?

**System Experience (20 minutes)**
4. Walk me through your experience using the AI recommendation system.
5. What was your first impression of the system?
6. How did the recommendations compare to resources you would typically choose?
7. Can you describe a specific instance where the system was particularly helpful?
8. Were there times when the recommendations didn't meet your needs? Can you elaborate?
9. How did you feel about the system's ability to adapt to your learning progress?
10. What impact, if any, did the system have on your motivation to learn?

**Specific Features (15 minutes)**
11. Which features of the system did you find most valuable? Why?
12. Were there any features you found confusing or unnecessary?
13. How important was it for you to understand why certain recommendations were made?
14. What did you think about the system's user interface and design?

**Broader Implications (10 minutes)**
15. How do you see AI recommendation systems fitting into education more broadly?
16. What concerns, if any, do you have about using AI in learning?
17. Would you want to continue using this type of system in future courses?
18. What advice would you give to other students considering using such a system?

**Wrap-up (5 minutes)**
19. Is there anything else about your experience that you'd like to share?
20. Do you have any questions about the research or the system?

### B.2 Focus Group Discussion Guide

**Opening (10 minutes)**
- Introductions and ground rules
- Overview of discussion topics
- Consent and confidentiality reminders

**General Reactions (15 minutes)**
1. What were your overall impressions of the AI recommendation system?
2. How did it compare to other learning tools you've used?
3. What surprised you most about the experience?

**Comparative Discussion (20 minutes)**
4. How did your experience with AI recommendations differ from traditional resource selection?
5. In what ways did the system change your learning approach?
6. Did anyone have significantly different experiences? What might explain these differences?

**Feature Analysis (20 minutes)**
7. Which system features generated the most discussion among your peers?
8. Were there features that some found helpful while others found problematic?
9. How important is transparency in AI recommendations for learning?

**Future Perspectives (15 minutes)**
10. How do you envision AI recommendation systems evolving in education?
11. What would make you more likely to adopt such systems in the future?
12. What concerns should educators and developers address?

**Closing (10 minutes)**
13. What key message would you want developers of these systems to hear?
14. Any final thoughts or experiences to share?

### C.2 Recommendation Engine Details

**Algorithm Components**

*Collaborative Filtering*
- Matrix factorization using Singular Value Decomposition (SVD)
- User-based and item-based nearest neighbor approaches
- Implicit feedback processing for engagement metrics

*Content-Based Filtering*
- TF-IDF vectorization of learning materials
- Cosine similarity calculations
- Feature extraction from multimedia content

*Hybrid Approach*
- Weighted combination of collaborative and content-based methods
- Dynamic weight adjustment based on data availability
- Cold start problem mitigation strategies

**Performance Metrics**
- Precision@K: 0.73 (K=10)
- Recall@K: 0.68 (K=10)
- Mean Absolute Error (MAE): 0.82
- Root Mean Square Error (RMSE): 1.15
- Coverage: 89.3%
- Diversity (Intra-List Diversity): 0.76

### C.3 Personalization Features

**Learning Style Adaptation**
- Visual, auditory, and kinesthetic content preferences
- Sequential vs. random learning path preferences
- Individual vs. collaborative learning preferences

**Difficulty Adjustment**
- Item Response Theory (IRT) for ability estimation
- Dynamic difficulty scaling based on performance
- Prerequisite knowledge tracking

**Temporal Patterns**
- Optimal study time identification
- Spaced repetition scheduling
- Deadline and workload management

## Appendix G: Additional Resources

### G.1 Glossary of Terms

**Artificial Intelligence (AI)**: Computer systems able to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as pattern recognition, decision-making, and learning.

**Collaborative Filtering**: A recommendation technique that makes predictions about user preferences based on the preferences of similar users.

**Content-Based Filtering**: A recommendation approach that suggests items similar to those a user has previously liked or interacted with.

**Learning Analytics**: The measurement, collection, analysis, and reporting of data about learners and their contexts for purposes of understanding and optimizing learning.

**Machine Learning**: A subset of AI that enables systems to automatically learn and improve from experience without being explicitly programmed.

**Personalized Learning**: Educational approaches that tailor instruction, content, and pace to individual learner needs, preferences, and interests.

**Recommendation System**: A software tool that suggests relevant items to users based on their preferences, behavior, or characteristics.

**User Experience (UX)**: The overall experience a person has when interacting with a system, including usability, accessibility, and satisfaction.
