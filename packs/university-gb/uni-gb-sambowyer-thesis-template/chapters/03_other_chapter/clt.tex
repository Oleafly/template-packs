\section{The Central Limit Theorem}
\label{clt:sec:clt}

\begin{equation}
    \sqrt{N} (\hat{\mu} - \mu) \xrightarrow{d} \mathcal{N} \left( 0, \sigma^2 \right) \; \text{as } N \rightarrow \infty, \label{clt:eq:CLT}
%\vspace{-2pt}
\end{equation}
where $\hat{\mu} = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N X_i$ is the sample mean.

This was first proved by \citet{clt}.

Also see \cref{app:clt_app:sec:appendix} for more details.
