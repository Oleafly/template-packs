\chapter*{AI Acknowledgement Statement}

This is an example of an AI Acknowledgement Statement.

This statement discloses the use of artificial intelligence tools during the preparation of this thesis.

\minisec{Writing}

AI-assisted tools were used to support the writing process. Grammarly \citep{Grammarly2025} was used for spelling and grammar checking throughout the writing process. Additionally, Claude Sonnet 4.5 \citep{Anthropic2025Sonnet} and Claude Opus 4.6 \citep{Anthropic2026Opus}  were used for proofreading, helping to identify inconsistencies and duplications. 
Claude Opus 4.6 \citep{Anthropic2026Opus} was used for LaTeX formatting.
All suggestions were reviewed and then accepted or rejected by the author. The intellectual content and conclusions remain entirely the author's own work.

\minisec{Coding}

AI-assisted tools were used during the development of the code written for this thesis. Claude Sonnet 4.5 \citep{Anthropic2025Sonnet} and Claude Opus 4.6 \citep{Anthropic2026Opus} were used to assist with linting and bug fixing. All AI-generated code suggestions were reviewed and tested by the author before inclusion.
