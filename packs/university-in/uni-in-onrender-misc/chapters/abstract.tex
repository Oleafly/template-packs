This LaTeX file was originally written while I was completing my PhD at the Indian Statistical Institute, Bangalore. After defending my thesis, I decided to convert it into a general-purpose template that can be used by anyone writing a thesis in LaTeX, and especially by ISI PhD scholars. The template includes a structured preamble, theorem environments, macros, color schemes, symbol and acronym management, and sample chapters.  

It is designed to be flexible and easily adaptable, allowing users to focus on writing their thesis content without worrying about formatting. By following the examples in this template, scholars can create professional-looking theses with consistent styling, automated lists of symbols and acronyms, and well-formatted mathematical content.  

This template aims to provide a practical starting point for LaTeX-based thesis writing while showcasing best practices for organizing large LaTeX projects.
