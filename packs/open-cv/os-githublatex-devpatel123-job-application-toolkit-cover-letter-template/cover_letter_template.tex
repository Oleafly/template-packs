% Cover letter template

%
% Compile with:  tectonic cover_letter_template.tex
%
% Target 250-300 words for industry roles. A character paragraph usually
% pushes this to ~310-330, which is fine.
% See docs/WRITING_RULES.md before writing.
%-------------------------

\documentclass[11pt,a4paper]{article}
\usepackage[T1]{fontenc}
\usepackage[utf8]{inputenc}
\usepackage{newpxtext}           % Palatino. Reads well in prose at letter length.
\usepackage{newpxmath}
\usepackage[margin=2.5cm]{geometry}
\usepackage[dvipsnames]{xcolor}
\usepackage[hidelinks]{hyperref}
\usepackage{parskip}
\pagestyle{empty}
\setlength{\parindent}{0pt}
\linespread{1.05}

\begin{document}

% Same header as the CV.
\begin{center}
    \textbf{\Huge FULL NAME} \\ \vspace{2pt}
    \small ROLE TITLE YOU ARE APPLYING FOR \\ \vspace{3pt}
    \small
    City, Country $|$ 000 000 0000 $|$
    \href{mailto:you@example.com}{\underline{you@example.com}} $|$
    \href{https://www.linkedin.com/in/yourprofile/}{\underline{LinkedIn}}
\end{center}

\vspace{6pt}
\today

\vspace{8pt}
Hiring Manager\\
Company Name

\vspace{10pt}
Dear Hiring Manager,

\vspace{6pt}

% PARAGRAPH 1 - COMPANY-SPECIFIC HOOK
% Never a generic opener. Reference what this company actually does or builds,
% and connect it to something genuinely true about you. Name the role.
% If you have a real point of connection (a relevant project, a domain overlap),
% lead with it here.
Open with what this company does and why it connects to you specifically, then name the
role you are applying for. One concrete link beats three sentences of enthusiasm.

% PARAGRAPH 2 - EVIDENCE
% Your strongest relevant work, with specifics and numbers where they exist.
% Reframe real projects toward this JD's language. Do not introduce claims
% that are not supported by your CV.
Describe the most relevant thing you have actually built or done, with enough detail that
it is clearly real: what the problem was, what you did, and how it turned out. Then a
second piece of evidence covering a different capability the role asks for.

% PARAGRAPH 3 - CHARACTER (required)
% Who you are, not what you can do. Pick 2-3 traits from your character bank and
% vary which ones across letters so they do not read templated. Keep it first
% person and specific.
Two things about how I work matter more to me than any single skill. [First trait, phrased
in your own words, with a reason.] And [second trait, ideally evidenced by something
long-running or verifiable].

% PARAGRAPH 4 - CLOSE
% Active call to action. Mention availability only if genuinely relevant
% (part-time roles, fixed start dates, internships).
I would welcome the chance to talk about the role. Thank you for your time.

\vspace{10pt}
Kind regards,\\[6pt]
FULL NAME

\end{document}
