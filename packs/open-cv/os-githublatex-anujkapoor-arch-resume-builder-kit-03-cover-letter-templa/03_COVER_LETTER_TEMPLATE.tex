% --- Paragraph 1: HOOK (why this company/role specifically) ---
<<2-4 sentences. Mirror the JD's language. Say what about the role/company
matches what you are looking for next. Avoid "I am passionate about..." --
be specific. Example:
"X is hiring for the exact profile I have spent the last six years becoming.
A self-sufficient AE who owns a US book end to end, self-sources pipeline,
closes in a fast SMB motion, and lives in HubSpot, ZoomInfo, Apollo, and
LinkedIn Sales Navigator. That is my day, and I would like it to be my next role.">>

% --- Paragraph 2: PROOF (your strongest recent quantified result) ---
<<3-5 sentences. Pick ONE recent result and tell the short version of it
with numbers. Use the exact deal name, exact percentage, exact ARR. Connect
the proof to a JD requirement. Example:
"The most direct proof is what I am doing right now. I joined X in Mon YYYY
as a quota-carrying SDM, with no SDR support, in an industry I had not sold
into before. I completed product certification in one week (27/30), closed
my first deal inside month one, and delivered 3.25x of quota in Q1 (\$155K
against \$48K target). Their SMB segment description reads like the exact
environment I just ramped into.">>

% --- Paragraph 3: DEPTH (the broader career arc) ---
<<3-5 sentences. Show your range. Cover your most relevant 2-3 prior roles.
Mention scale (\$ ARR, regions, named accounts). Connect to the JD's range.
Example:
"Before X, I spent ~5 years at Y carrying a \$500K ARR quota across the US,
Middle East, and SEA. Earlier I built Y's SEA region from zero and self-sourced
a \$268K close. A.ai and B before that gave me zero-to-one startup discipline
and a heavy outbound SMB motion. I have run the established-software playbook
and the scrappy early-stage one, and X is the right point on that curve.">>

% --- Paragraph 4: HONESTY (gap + bridge) ---
<<2-4 sentences. Name the one gap directly and explain why it is not a
dealbreaker. This is the single most credible thing you can do in a cover
letter. Example:
"What I do not bring is direct GRC or compliance domain experience. What I
do bring is six years of selling regulated, sensitive-data products to the
same SMB buyer X sells to: CHROs, finance leaders, and operations heads who
care about data, vendor risk, and audit-readiness. I would back myself to
ramp into the product fast, the way I did into healthcare in week one at my current employer.">>

% --- Closing ---
<<One line. Example: "Would love a 30-minute conversation. Thank you for reading.">>
